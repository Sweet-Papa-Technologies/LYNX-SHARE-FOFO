## Highlights
- The fork now numbers its releases by week: 26.47.1 is the release of week 47 of 2026. It follows 0.1.27, and the in-app update check treats it as the newer version.
- This release brings in upstream LYNX SHARE's work up to its 0.1.30.
- Recording a new take in Edison puts it after the previous take, not in front of it.
- The updater has a Stable and a Beta channel, so nightly betas are not offered to people on stable builds.
- When LYNX ends by a C++ runtime failure (an exception nobody catches, abort, a pure virtual call, an invalid C runtime parameter), it leaves a crash file that says what happened.
